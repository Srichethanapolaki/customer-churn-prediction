### 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### Overview
Exploratory Data Analysis is performed on the Telco Customer Churn dataset to understand customer behavior, identify patterns associated with churn, and support the development of a machine learning model.

### Objectives
- Analyze customer churn distribution.
- Investigate the relationship between contract type and churn.
- Understand how customer tenure relates to churn.
- Compare monthly charges for customers who stayed and those who left.
- Analyze churn across technical support and payment method categories.
- Identify potential customer retention opportunities.

### Data Analysis Tasks
1. Inspect dataset dimensions, data types, and missing values.
2. Clean and prepare relevant columns.
3. Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
4. Visualize churn patterns across customer segments.
5. Summarize business insights and recommendations.

### Visualizations
The analysis includes:
- Overall customer churn distribution.
- Churn by contract type.
- Customer tenure distribution by churn status.
- Churn rate by tenure group.
- Monthly charges by churn status.
- Churn by technical support.
- Churn by payment method.

### Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

### Expected Outcome
The analysis helps identify customer segments associated with higher churn and provides useful insights for building and evaluating a machine learning model.

### Business Applications
- Improve customer onboarding and engagement.
- Investigate service issues associated with customer departures.
- Develop targeted retention strategies.
- Support data-driven customer retention decisions.
